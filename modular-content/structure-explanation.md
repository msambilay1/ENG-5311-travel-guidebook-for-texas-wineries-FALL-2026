# Content Structure

We've broken up the Austin Winery's section into its building blocks, and prefaced each section with an "aw" to denote that it belongs to the Austin Winery. Any future sections restructured in this way will have the same titles, but with their initials prefacing them. 

## Destination Overview

The destination overview was broken up into three sections:
- aw-description.md: The main body test that provides an overview for the winery.
- aw-operating-hours.md: The hours of operation table.
- aw-lodging.md: The list of lodging options near the winery.

The navigation section was broken up into three sections:
- aw-location-overview.md: The location snippet of the winery.
- aw-map.md: The map image with its address.
- aw-driving-directions.md: The detailed driving directions to navigate to the winery.

## Wine Catalogue:

The wine catalogue was broken up into its individual wine entries, which allows for easier revision. Sections were titled according to wine names.
