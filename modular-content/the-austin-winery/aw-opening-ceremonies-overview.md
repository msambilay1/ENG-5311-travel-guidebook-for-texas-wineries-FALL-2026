### Rosé

61% Vermentino, Salt Lick Vineyards | 
39% Cabernet Sauvignon, One Elm |

Fermented in the traditional French saignée style.
Notes of _white strawberry, Asian pear, soft Mediterranean herbs._

Vermentino’s crisp acidity and minerality make it an ideal companion for seafood dishes. Cabernet Sauvignon has high tannins, firm structure, and dark fruit notes. Foods with fat, protein, and umami soften the tannins, while savory or spicy elements echo the wine’s peppery and herbal undertones

Food pairings: Sea bass, shellfish, veal for proteins; Comte, Parmesan, Mozzarella for cheeses; Caprese salad, herbal pastas, citrus and nutty-based salad dressings for vegetarian options
