There are plenty of options for great wine around Austin, but The Austin Winery is the only full-production winery in city limits. Owned and operated by a young team of winemakers, this urban winery produces all natural wines from all Texas grapes.

Off of South Congress, The Austin Winery is in a cozy industrial space in The Yard business park. The functional production facility keeps the space cool all summer long, and gives you a chance to see the winemaking process from your table as tanks and barrels line the tasting room.

Tasting flights are available at $20 for your pick of 3 glasses.

For food options, just out their front door you’ll find Polvo’s Taco Truck, Reem’s Jerusalem Street Food, and For Cod’s Sake fish and chips. If you arrive early, Nougatine Bakery also operates daily within The Austin Winery space, 7:30 AM - 2 PM.
