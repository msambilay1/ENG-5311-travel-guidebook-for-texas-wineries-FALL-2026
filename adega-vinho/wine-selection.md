### Rosé

100% Mourvèdre, Parr Vineyards | Hand harvested, fermented and aged in French Oak barrels.

Notes of _floral, fresh, supple_.

Mourvèdre is full-bodied, with flavors of plum, blackberry, dark chocolate, black pepper, smoke, and dried herbs. It has more structure and tannin than lighter rosés, so it matches well with robust, flavorful foods rather than delicate, sweet, or overly light dishes. 

Food pairings: lamb, beef and veal, and venison for proteins; smoked Cheddar, aged Provolone, and Parmesan for cheeses; eggplant, mushroom-based stir fries, and lentils for vegetarian options

Price: $35/bottle

## ![2020 Tempranillo](https://store.adegavinho.com/2020-tempranillo-parr-p182.aspx)
<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/adega-vinho/wines/tempranillo-parr.jpg?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### Red

100% Tempranillo, Parr Vineyards | Hand-harvested, fermented and aged in oak barrels.

Notes of _bright cherry, leather, plum_.

Tempranillo is a medium-to-full-bodied red with moderate acidity and firm tannins, featuring flavors of plum, dried strawberry, earthy leather, tobacco, and subtle oak notes in aged wines. Its structure allows it to complement protein-rich and savory dishes, while its acidity balances fatty meats and tomato-based sauces.

Food pairings: Lamb chops, pork tenderloin, and chorizo for proteins; Manchego, Gruyere, Pecorino Romano for cheeses; roasted red peppers, mushrooms, and tomato-based sauces for vegetarian options


Price: $50/bottle


## ![2023 Estate Rose](https://store.adegavinho.com/2023-ros-estate-p180.aspx)
<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/adega-vinho/wines/rose-estate.jpg?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### Rose

50% Tempranillo, 50% Mourvèdre, Bilger Family Vineyards | Hand harvested, whole cluster pressed then fermented and aged in a proprietary barrel system.

Notes of _strawberry, dried rose, hibiscus_.

Tempranillo is medium‑to‑full‑bodied, with red fruit (plum, cherry), earthy/tobacco notes, and moderate‑firm tannins. Mourvèdre is full‑bodied, with ripe red/black fruit, herbal, spice, and sometimes smoky or gamey notes. Rosé wines are typically light, fruity, and acidic, which allows them to complement both delicate and moderately flavorful dishes. 

Food pairings: Tuna, shrimp, and scallops for proteins; goat cheese, Feta, and burrata for cheeses; ratatouille, vegetable tarts, and quiches for vegetarian options

Price: $35/bottle

## ![Chardonnay 2023](https://store.adegavinho.com/2023-chardonnay-p41.aspx)

<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/adega-vinho/wines/chardonnay.jpg?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### White

100% Chardonnay, Dell Valley Vineyards | Fermented and aged in a combination of new French oak, neutral oak and stainless steel for 9 months.

Notes of _citrus zest, apple, cream_.

Chardonnay pairs beautifully with a wide range of foods, with unoaked styles complementing lighter dishes and oaked styles enhancing richer, buttery flavors.
Food pairings: Chicken, turkey breast, meaty fish like halibut for proteins; Brie, Camembert, fresh Mozzarella for cheeses; lemon garlic linguine, pasta primavera, custard-based desserts for vegetarian options

Price: $45/bottle
