# Navigating to Adega Vinho

Located just east of Fredericksburg, on the Highway 290 corridor, sits Adega Vinho among the expansive Texas Wine Country. 

<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/maps/adega-vinho.png?raw=true" width="600" align="center">

## Coming from Fredericksburg?
If you're coming from Fredericksburg, take U.S. Highway 290 East toward Stonewall. Continue on U.S. Highway 290 through Stonewall, then turn right onto Ranch to Market Road 1623. Follow RM 1623 south and continue to 1000 RM 1623, where Adega Vinho is located.

## Coming from Austin?
If you're coming from Austin, take U.S. Highway 290 West through Dripping Springs and Johnson City. Continue on U.S. Highway 290 toward Stonewall, then turn left onto Ranch to Market Road 1623. Follow RM 1623 south to 1000 RM 1623 and Adega Vinho.

## Coming from San Antonio?
If you're coming from San Antonio, take I-10 West toward Boerne and Comfort. Take U.S. Highway 87 North toward Fredericksburg and continue through Comfort on Front Street. Continue to Fredericksburg, then turn right onto U.S. Highway 290 East toward Stonewall. Turn right onto Ranch to Market Road 1623 and follow it south to 1000 RM 1623.
