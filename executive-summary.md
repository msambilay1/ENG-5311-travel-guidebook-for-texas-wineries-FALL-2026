Executive Overview

We are working on a travel guidebook for vineyards located in the Texas Hill Country area. This guidebook is intended to provide an all-in-one overview for travelers to reduce the need to refer to multiple websites and sources.
The travel guidebook is comprised of 6 vineyards spanning the area between Boerne, TX and Austin, TX. The vineyards were selected based on proximity to common highways in the Hill Country so as to facilitate transportation. Overall, we selected vineyards located in Austin, Wimberley, New Braunfels, Stonewall, Boerne, and Fredericksburg. These areas were selected as they are easy for travelers to get to via highways; this is assuming that the traveler is based in San Marcos, TX. 
The guidebook includes important information for the traveler to know, such as hours of operation, address, and directions to the vineyard. It also includes some introductory information about the vineyard in the case that the traveler cannot access the vineyard's website and social media. 
The guidebook includes 5-6 notable wine options for each vineyard and a link to purchase the wine online if the traveler would like to purchase a bottle any time after visiting the vineyard. The wine options offer a flavor profile, some information about the varietal (grape) used in the wine, and recommended food pairings such as entrees, cheeses, and vegetarian options for the traveler's enjoyment.
The guidebook also provides a selection of nearby lodging options should the traveler choose to spend the day at the vineyard and travel the day after. The lodging options include address information, approximate rates for the night, and a link to the booking tool for the selected lodging. These options were selected by determining proximity of 10 miles or less to the vineyard and span a number of price rates depending on the traveler's budget.
The guidebook's purpose is to inform travelers of local vineyards, wine selections, and lodging options thereby reducing the traveler's need to scour many websites and social media outlets to be able to plan a day trip to a Hill Country vineyard.
