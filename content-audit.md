# Content Audit

## Incomplete Information
1. **Winery information** - To have the complete guidebook, we should include more robust information of
each winery to include their location, prices, history, operating hours, events,
selection, and nearby accommodations/dining/entertainment/etc
2. **Visual Assets** - We are still missing photographs, maps, directions, and accompanying captions.
3. **Timeline Accuracy** - We could consider having a calendar that incorporates future events or other
information that will change over time.

## List of Unresolved Questions for Users, Clients, or Stakeholders
1. What information do travelers consider the most important when choosing a
winery? And if we can determine that, how do we organize our guidebook to
target that?
2. How would travelers prefer our wineries to be organized? Geographically,
alphabetically, or in categories based on experience types?
3. Would our target audience prefer concise details with essential information or
would they prefer a more detailed description of the wineries?
4. What information would help determine a winery is suitable for a group's needs?
Such as accessibility, group size, transportation, accommodations, etc.
