# Notable Wines

## Mourvèdre 2024
<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/the-austin-winery/wines/mourvedre.jpg?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### Red
93% Mourvèdre, Salt Lick Vineyards | 
7% Alicante Bouschet, Salt Lick Vineyards |

Field blend fermented whole-cluster.
Notes of _plum, cedar, wild herbs._

Mourvèdre is full-bodied, with flavors of plum, blackberry, dark chocolate, black pepper, smoke, and dried herbs. Its firm tannins and savory character make it ideal for high-protein, high-fat, and flavorful dishes that can stand up to its intensity.

Food pairings: Lamb, beef, venison for proteins; smoked cheddar, smoked gouda, aged provolone for cheese; eggplant and lentils for vegetarian options.

$13/glass | $35/bottle


## Acqua Pazza
<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/the-austin-winery/wines/acqua-pazza.jpg?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### White

55% Albariño, One Elm Vineyards |
32% Viognier, Paradis Vineyards |
13% Vermentino, One Elm Vineyards |

Fermented on Sauvignon Blanc & Muscat skins.
Notes of _nectarine, peach skin, citrus zest._

Albariño’s high acidity, delicate fruit, and natural salinity make it a perfect match for seafood. Viognier’s stone fruit and floral notes balance the richness of seafood, while its subtle acidity cuts through creamy or buttery sauces  

Food pairings: Oysters, clams, white fish for proteins; Manchego, Feta, Comte, and Burrata for cheeses; elote, watermelon, and herb sauce-based dishes for vegetarian options

$12/glass | $32/bottle

### Rosé

61% Vermentino, Salt Lick Vineyards | 
39% Cabernet Sauvignon, One Elm |

Fermented in the traditional French saignée style.
Notes of _white strawberry, Asian pear, soft Mediterranean herbs._

Vermentino’s crisp acidity and minerality make it an ideal companion for seafood dishes. Cabernet Sauvignon has high tannins, firm structure, and dark fruit notes. Foods with fat, protein, and umami soften the tannins, while savory or spicy elements echo the wine’s peppery and herbal undertones

Food pairings: Sea bass, shellfish, veal for proteins; Comte, Parmesan, Mozzarella for cheeses; Caprese salad, herbal pastas, citrus and nutty-based salad dressings for vegetarian options


$14/glass | $40/bottle
