# Navigating to The Austin Winery

## Coming from the South?
Get off of I-35 North through exit **228**. Continue along the frontage road until you get to E William Cannon Drive. Turn left onto East William Cannon Drive, then turn right onto North Bluff Drive. Turn right onto South Congress Avenue and continue until you get to East St Elmo Road. Turn right onto East St Elmo Road, and continue straight until you see The Austin Winery on your left.

## Coming from the North?
Get off of I-35 North through exit **229**. Continue along the frontage road, then turn right onto Colonial Park Boulevard. Continue straight onto Terry-O Lane, then turn left onto E St Elmo Road. You should see The Austin Winery on your right.
