# The Texas Hill Country AVA

At over nine million acres, the Texas Hill Country is the third-largest American Viticultural Area (AVA) in the nation. AVAs are the regulated areas of significant wine production, and the Texas Hill Country is one of the largest and also one of the most unique. With the Chihuahuan Desert to the south, the Coastal Plains to the east, and the prairies to the west and north, the rolling hills of Central Texas have a great diversity in climate, and by extension, a great diversity in wine.
