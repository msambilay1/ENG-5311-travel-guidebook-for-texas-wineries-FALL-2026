Saint Tryphon is a small, low-intervention winery set on an 1890s homestead in the Hill Country outside Boerne. Every wine is made from 100% Texas-grown grapes. Most are fermented with native yeast and bottled unfined and unfiltered, with only a small amount of sulfur dioxide added.

The tasting room and covered porch are open to visitors, along with the first two rows of the vineyard. First-time visitors are welcome to walk the grounds and see the historic homestead. The area behind the tasting room is reserved for wine club members and is clearly marked.

Tastings cost $25 per person and include five 1 oz pours chosen by the winemaker, which adds up to about one full glass. Tastings are for guests 21 and older.

Outside food is permitted, and barbecue may be eaten outdoors only. The winery also sells charcuterie, cheeses, cured meats, olives, nuts, chocolate, and tinned fish on site. Outside alcohol is not permitted.
