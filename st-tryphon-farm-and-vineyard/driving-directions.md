# Navigating to Saint Tryphon Farm and Vineyard

Located in the Hill Country outside Boerne, northwest of San Antonio near the Sisterdale area. The winery is approximately 30 minutes from San Antonio and provides a rural stop along the Hill Country wine route.

<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/maps/st-tryphon.png?raw=true" width="600" align="center">

> 24 Wasp Creek Rd, Boerne, TX 78006

## Coming from Boerne?
If you're coming from Boerne, take Highway 46 East, then turn onto North Main Street/Business U.S. Highway 87. Follow North Main Street north and turn onto Ranch to Market Road 1376/Sisterdale Road. Continue on RM 1376, then turn onto Wasp Creek Road. Follow Wasp Creek Road to 24 Wasp Creek Road.

## Coming from Fredericksburg?
If you're coming from Fredericksburg, take U.S. Highway 87 South toward Comfort. Continue through Comfort on Front Street, then turn onto Ranch to Market Road 473 East toward Sisterdale. Continue to Ranch to Market Road 1376, turn south, and follow RM 1376 toward Boerne. Turn onto Wasp Creek Road and follow it to 24 Wasp Creek Road.

## Coming from San Antonio?
If you're coming from San Antonio, take I-10 West toward Boerne and take the exit for Main Street/Business U.S. Highway 87. Continue on South Main Street, which becomes North Main Street, and follow it north out of Boerne. Turn onto Ranch to Market Road 1376/Sisterdale Road, then turn onto Wasp Creek Road. Follow Wasp Creek Road to 24 Wasp Creek Road.
