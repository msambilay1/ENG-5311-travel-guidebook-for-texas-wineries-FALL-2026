### Red

Mourvedre, Merlot, Tannat

Notes of _cherry, red plum, dried thyme, warm stone_.

Mourvèdre is full-bodied, with flavors of plum, blackberry, dark chocolate, black pepper, smoke, and dried herbs. Merlot consists of medium to full body, soft tannins, and fruit-forward profile. Tannat’s bold structure and dark fruit, cocoa, tobacco, and leather notes match well with equally bold, savory dishes. 

Food pairings: Lamb, duck, and wild boar for proteins; Comte, Provolone, mountain cheese for cheeses; mushroom, eggplant, and black olive-based entrees for vegetarian options

Price: $44/bottle

### Rose

[placeholder]

Notes of _berry, plum, cream_.

Rose Tannat is a rare, tannic, and full‑bodied red wine style that can be enjoyed as a rosé‑like or pale‑red wine, often with notes of dark fruit, cocoa, tobacco, and earthy spice. Its structure and depth make it ideal for flavorful, hearty, and slightly robust dishes, while its acidity and fruitiness also allow for lighter, fresh pairings. 

Food pairings: Entrecôte steak, ribeye, and wild boar for proteins; Comte, mountain cheese, and Manchego for cheeses; lentils, grilled root vegetables, and tomato-basil salads for vegetarian options

Price: $38/bottle
