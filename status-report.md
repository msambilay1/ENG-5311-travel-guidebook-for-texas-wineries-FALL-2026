# Status Report

## Executive Overview

We are working on a travel guidebook for vineyards located in the Texas Hill Country area. This guidebook is intended to provide an all-in-one overview for travelers to reduce the need to refer to multiple websites and sources.

The travel guidebook is comprised of 6 vineyards spanning the area between Boerne, TX and Austin, TX. The vineyards were selected based on proximity to common highways in the Hill Country so as to facilitate transportation. Overall, we selected vineyards located in Austin, Wimberley, New Braunfels, Stonewall, Boerne, and Fredericksburg. These areas were selected as they are easy for travelers to get to via highways; this is assuming that the traveler is based in San Marcos, TX. 

The guidebook includes important information for the traveler to know, such as hours of operation, address, and directions to the vineyard. It also includes some introductory information about the vineyard in the case that the traveler cannot access the vineyard's website and social media. 

The guidebook includes 5-6 notable wine options for each vineyard and a link to purchase the wine online if the traveler would like to purchase a bottle any time after visiting the vineyard. The wine options offer a flavor profile, some information about the varietal (grape) used in the wine, and recommended food pairings such as entrees, cheeses, and vegetarian options for the traveler's enjoyment.

The guidebook also provides a selection of nearby lodging options should the traveler choose to spend the day at the vineyard and travel the day after. The lodging options include address information, approximate rates for the night, and a link to the booking tool for the selected lodging. These options were selected by determining proximity of 10 miles or less to the vineyard and span a number of price rates depending on the traveler's budget.

The guidebook's purpose is to inform travelers of local vineyards, wine selections, and lodging options thereby reducing the traveler's need to scour many websites and social media outlets to be able to plan a day trip to a Hill Country vineyard.

## Project Status, Recommendations, and Rationale

### Current status:
The Texas Hill Country Travel Guidebook has finished its initial development and usability testing. The guidebook has been developed to provide travelers with a centralized resource for planning visits to the vineyards throughout the Texas Hill Country, with the goal of reducing the need to consult multiple sources to plan one trip. 

The guidebook, as it stands, includes six vineyards located in Austin, Wimberley, New Braunfels, Stonewall, Boerne, and Fredericksburg. Each vineyard section provides essential information, such as operating hours, addresses, directions, and introductory descriptions. Additionally, the guidebook includes wine catalogs featuring approximately five to six selections per vineyard, flavor profiles, grape varietal information, food pairings, and purchasing links. Nearby lodging recommendations are also provided, including approximate nightly rates, addresses, and booking information.

With this draft, the team conducted usability testing to evaluate the guidebook's effectiveness in travel planning. Participants were asked to complete five related tasks involving the development of a mock itinerary using the guidebook. During testing, participants were encouraged to verbalize their thought processes while observers documented their experiences and any difficulties encountered. Overall, the usability testing indicated that the guidebook successfully conveyed information about the individual vineyards and their wine selections. Participants generally found these sections helpful and were able to use the information to complete the associated tasks. 

However, testing also revealed opportunities to improve the guidebook's usefulness as a comprehensive one-stop resource. 

### Recommendations and Rationale:
Based on the usability testing findings, it is recommended that we prioritize improvements to travel logistics, dining recommendations and pairings, and document accessibility. Based on user feedback, the guidebook should include more specifics that would eliminate the need for external resources.The team's recommendations, therefore, is to focus on refining and expanding existing content rather than restructuring the guidebook. By incorporating more detailed travel logistics, additional dining recommendations, and accessibility improvements, the guidebook can better fulfill its intended purpose as a holistic travel resource.

## Next Steps

The team should begin by reviewing our existing travel related logistics information such as lodging and transportation and identify where additional information would benefit readers. This is grounded in the usability feedback where users suggested adding approximate driving distances or tavel times between the vineyard and its associated lodging/outside activities.

The team should expand the dining information associated to the nearby options of the vineyards. This could look like suggested pairings, proximity considerations, and pricing for the best paired meal for the wine

The team should review the guidebooks formatting to identify and correct the navigation problems observed during usability testing. Specifically, the PDF formatting issues, the table of contents working improperly, and the images lacking captions as noted by users.

4. Integrate revisions and review
The team should work to incorporate the revisions suggested from the usability testing and do a final review.
