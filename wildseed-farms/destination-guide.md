## Wildseed Farms

Wildseed Farms offers a charmingly different take on Texas Hill Country wine country: a working wildflower farm, vineyard, gardens, and tasting room all in one destination.

The tasting room is a great stop for visitors who want to pair wine tasting with a little outdoor exploration. 

Visitors enjoy the wildflower and butterfly gardens, the half-mile walking trail, and the gift shop stocked with seeds, pottery, home décor, and local treats. Wine enthusiasts appreciate being able to sample the farm’s wines as part of a broader day out. Note that the flowers are highly seasonal and the property can be quieter outside peak bloom periods.

They offer a five-pour dry wine tasting or sweet wine tasting for $25 per person.
