# Notable Wines

## ![2021 Tempranillo](https://wildseedfarms.com/collections/shop-wine/products/2021-tempranillo)

<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/wildseed-farms/wines/tempranillo.jpg?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### Red

Notes of _black cherry, dried fig, leather, sweet tobacco, cloves_.

Tempranillo is a medium-to-full-bodied red with moderate acidity and firm tannins, featuring flavors of plum, dried strawberry, earthy leather, tobacco, and subtle oak notes in aged wines. Its structure allows it to complement protein-rich and savory dishes, while its acidity balances fatty meats and tomato-based sauces.

Food pairings: Lamb chops, pork tenderloin, and chorizo for proteins; Manchego, Gruyere, Pecorino Romano for cheeses; roasted red peppers, mushrooms, and tomato-based sauces for vegetarian options

Price: $40/bottle

## ![2023 Viognier](https://wildseedfarms.com/collections/shop-wine/products/2023-viognier)

<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/wildseed-farms/wines/viognier.jpg?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### White

Notes of _apricot, peach, honeysuckle_.

Although a dry white wine, Viognier has subtle sweet notes that blend wonderfully with its distinctive aromas. When you smell it, you immediately get the aromas of tangerine, white flowers, but dominantly peach and apricot. And if Viognier is aged in oak barrels, the aromas are amplified.

Food pairing: Chicken, turkey, oily fish for proteins; Brie, aged Cheddar, Halloumi for cheeses; butternut squash, sweet potato, and pumpkin for vegetarian options

Price: $35/bottle


## ![2023 Hill Country Espumosa](https://wildseedfarms.com/collections/shop-wine/products/espumosa)

<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/wildseed-farms/wines/hill-country-espumosa.jpg?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### Sparkling

Notes of _pear, Lychee, green apple_.

Espumosa is a crisp, refreshing sparkling wine with bright citrus and fruit notes, lively acidity, and a medium body. Its effervescence and light structure make it ideal for pairing with delicate, fresh, or slightly sweet dishes.

Food pairings: Shellfish, white fish, cod for proteins; Brie, Parmesan, and Grana Padano for cheeses; potato chips, crostini, and citrus-forward desserts for vegetarian options

Price: $42/bottle


## ![Peach Wine](https://wildseedfarms.com/collections/shop-wine/products/peach-wine)

<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/wildseed-farms/wines/peach-fruit.jpg?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### White

Notes of _peach, honey, and apricot_.

Peach wine is a light, sweet, and refreshing fruit wine made by fermenting ripe peaches with water, sugar, and wine yeast. It is often enjoyed chilled as a dessert wine and is known for its golden hue, smooth texture, and unmistakable stone‑fruit character.

Food pairings: Pork, ribs, and white fish for proteins; ricotta, Blue cheese, and Mozzarella for cheeses; heirloom tomato salad, Asian spice-based entrees, and citrus vinaigrettes for vegetarian options

Price: $24.95/bottle
