# Navigating to Wildseed Farms

Located just outside Fredericksburg in the heart of Texas wine country, east of town along the Highway 290 corridor. Its location makes it a convenient stop while exploring Fredericksburg and the surrounding Hill Country wineries.

<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/maps/wildseed.png?raw=true" width="600" align="center">

> 100 Legacy Dr, Fredericksburg, TX 78624

## From Fredericksburg?
If you're coming from Fredericksburg, take U.S. Highway 290 east toward the wineries and Stonewall. Continue past the edge of town, where you'll see vineyards and tasting rooms along the highway. Look for the signs for Wildseed Farms, then turn onto Legacy Drive and follow it to 100 Legacy Drive.
## From Austin?
If you're coming from Austin, take U.S. Highway 290 west toward Fredericksburg. The route passes through the Hill Country and the communities and wineries along the way. As you approach Fredericksburg, continue on U.S. Highway 290 and watch for signs for Wildseed Farms. Turn onto Legacy Drive and follow it to the farm.
## From San Antonio?
If you're coming from San Antonio, take I-10 west toward Kerrville, then follow U.S. Highway 87 north to Fredericksburg. Take U.S. Highway 290 east toward Stonewall and continue past the city limits. Follow the signs for Wildseed Farms, turn onto Legacy Drive, and continue to 100 Legacy Drive.
