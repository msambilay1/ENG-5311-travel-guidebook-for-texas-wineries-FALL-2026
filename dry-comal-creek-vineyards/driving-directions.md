#  Navigating to Dry Comal Creek Vineyards

Located just outside New Braunfels in the Texas Hill Country, a few miles northwest of the city near Highway 46. The vineyard is roughly between New Braunfels and Canyon Lake, making it an easy stop when traveling through the southern Hill Country.

<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/maps/dry-comal-creek.png?raw=true" width="600" align="center">

> 1741 Herbelin Rd, New Braunfels, TX 78132

## On I-35?
- If you're on I-35 North, take **exit 175**, turn left onto Roy Richard Drive and follow the road until you get to State Highway 46 West. Turn right onto Herbelin Road, then continue until you see your destination.
- If you're on I-35 South, take **exit 190B** onto Post Road, turn right onto Gruene Road, turn left onto Hanz Drive, and get into the right lane of Loop 337. Drive until you take the ramp onto State Highway 46 West. Continue on State Highway 46 West, then turn left onto Herbelin Road.

## On US-281?
- If you're coming from the North, drive on US-281 until you can turn left onto FM311. Drive along FM311 until you can merge right onto State Highway 46 West. Continue on State Highway 46 West, then turn right onto Herbelin Road.
- If you're coming from the South, drive on US-281 until you get to the ramp towards New Braunfels on the right lane. Turn right onto State Highway 46 West. Continue on State Highway 46 West, then turn right onto Herbelin Road.
