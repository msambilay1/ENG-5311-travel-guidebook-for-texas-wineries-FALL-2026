## Dry Comal Creek Vineyards

Just a few miles from New Braunfels, Dry Comal Creek Vineyards offers a relaxed Texas Hill Country experience centered on locally grown grapes and time spent outdoors. 

Winemaker Seth Urbanek brings experience from Cornell, France, and Australia, working closely with Texas grape growers to create distinctive varietals and blends.

Plan to linger over a bottle, share a charcuterie board or pizza, and check the events calendar for live music and food trucks. The vineyard is particularly well suited to those looking for an easygoing afternoon rather than a formal wine tour. 

> One important detail: this is an adults-only destination, with all guests required to be 21 or older.
