<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/dry-comal-creek/wines/tempranillo.png?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### Red

100% Tempranillo | Timmons Estate

Notes of _bright red fruit, mint, and tea_.

Tempranillo is a medium-to-full-bodied red with moderate acidity and firm tannins, featuring flavors of plum, dried strawberry, earthy leather, tobacco, and subtle oak notes in aged wines. Its structure allows it to complement protein-rich and savory dishes, while its acidity balances fatty meats and tomato-based sauces.

Food pairings: Lamb chops, pork tenderloin, and chorizo for proteins; Manchego, Gruyere, Pecorino Romano for cheeses; roasted red peppers, mushrooms, and tomato-based sauces for vegetarian options

Price: $42/bottle

## ![2024 Chenin Blanc](https://drycomalcreekvineyards.com/product/2024-chenin-blanc)
<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/dry-comal-creek/wines/chenen-blanc.png?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### White

Dell Valley Vineyards

Notes of persimmon, beeswax, and meyer lemon

Chenin Blanc is often known for its notes of honey and straw. Wines are also often described as having a perceptible minerality, as well as a palate that is rich in stone-fruit flavors. Other fruits that are often prominent on the nose and palate include green apple, guava, pear and quince. 

Food pairings: Shellfish, trout, and duck for proteins; goat cheese, Feta, and Comte for cheeses; pumpkin risotto, fennel salad, and asparagus with hollandaise for vegetarian options

Price: $32/bottle

## ![2023 Blanc of Blancs](https://drycomalcreekvineyards.com/product/blanc-de-blancs)
<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/dry-comal-creek/wines/blanc-de-blancs.png?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### Sparkling

100% Blanc de Blancs

Notes of brioche and passionfruit

Blanc de Blancs is a sparkling wine made from 100% white grapes (most often Chardonnay in Champagne) with citrus, apple, brioche, and almond notes, plus high acidity and fine bubbles. Its crisp, mineral-driven profile makes it versatile for seafood, light dishes, and certain cheeses. 

Food pairings: Oysters, lobster, and sea bass for proteins; Brie, Camembert, and Chaource for cheeses; vegetable quiche, souffles, and pasta with black olivers for vegetarian options

Price: $34/bottle (members only)
