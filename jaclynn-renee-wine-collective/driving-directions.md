# Navigating to the Jaclynn Renee Wine Collective

Located in the heart of Wimberley, southwest of Austin in the central Texas Hill Country. The tasting room is conveniently situated near Wimberley’s downtown shops, restaurants, and other local attractions.

<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/maps/jaclynn-renee.png?raw=true" width="600" align="center">

> 107 Henson Rd, Wimberley, TX 78676

## Coming from the North?
If you're coming from Austin, take U.S. Highway 290 West toward Dripping Springs. Turn left onto Ranch Road 12 South and follow it toward Wimberley. Continue on Ranch Road 12 into town and toward Wimberley Square. Turn onto Henson Road and follow it to 107 Henson Road.

## From San Marcos?
If you're coming from San Marcos, take East Hopkins Street toward Ranch Road 12. Continue onto Moore Street, then follow Oscar Smith Avenue toward Old Ranch Road 12. Turn onto Ranch Road 12 North and follow it into Wimberley. Near Wimberley Square, turn onto Henson Road and continue to 107 Henson Road.

## Coming from the West?
If you're coming from Fredericksburg, take U.S. Highway 290 East toward Johnson City. Continue through Johnson City and stay on U.S. Highway 290 toward Dripping Springs. Turn right onto Ranch Road 12 South and follow it toward Wimberley. Continue into town and toward Wimberley Square, then turn onto Henson Road. Follow Henson Road to 107 Henson Road.
