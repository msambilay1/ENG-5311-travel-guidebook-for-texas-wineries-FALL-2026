### Red

Texas High Plains Mourvèdre, Desert Willow Vineyard | Aged in 30% French oak

Notes of _dark cherry, vanilla spice, sage_.

Mourvèdre is full-bodied, with flavors of plum, blackberry, dark chocolate, black pepper, smoke, and dried herbs. Its firm tannins and savory character make it ideal for high-protein, high-fat, and flavorful dishes that can stand up to its intensity.

Food pairings: Lamb, beef, venison for proteins; smoked cheddar, smoked gouda, aged provolone for cheese; eggplant and lentils for vegetarian options.

Price: $48.00/bottle

### White

Dijon Clone 95, Sangiacomo Vineyard

Notes of _honeysuckle, baked apple, fresh pear, citrus notes_.

Chardonnay pairs beautifully with a wide range of foods, with unoaked styles complementing lighter dishes and oaked styles enhancing richer, buttery flavors.

Food pairings: Chicken, turkey breast, meaty fish like halibut for proteins; Brie, Camembert, fresh Mozzarella for cheeses; lemon garlic linguine, pasta primavera, custard-based desserts for vegetarian options

### Sparkling

Blanc de Blancs, Chardonnay, Pinot Blanc

Notes of _white blossom, citrus zest, crisp green apple_.

Chardonnay pairs beautifully with a wide range of foods, with unoaked styles complementing lighter dishes and oaked styles enhancing richer, buttery flavors. Lighter, unoaked Pinot Blanc (also called Pinot Bianco in Italy or Weissburgunder in Germany) is refreshing and crisp, with flavors of green apple, pear, citrus, and almond 

Food pairings: Shellfish, sole, light chicken entrees for proteins; Brie, Camembert, fresh Mozzarella for cheeses; crudites, pasta primavera, mildly spicy Asian entrees for vegetarian options


Price: $62.00/bottle


## ![2025 Grenache Rose](https://jaclynnreneewines.com/2025-grenache-rose/)
<img src="https://github.com/msambilay1/ENG-5311-travel-guidebook-for-texas-wineries-FALL-2026/blob/assets/images/jaclynn-renee/wines/grenache-rose.jpg?raw=true" width="150" align="right">

### Rose

Grenache, Desert Willow Vineyard

Notes of _red berry, citrus zest, minerals_.

Grenache is composed of ripe red‑fruit flavors, soft tannins, and warm, spicy profile. Grenache rosé typically offers strawberry, citrus, and floral notes with a balance of acidity and body. This means it can handle both light, fresh dishes and richer, spicier preparations without overpowering the food. 

Food pairings: Salmon, red snapper, and scallops for proteins; Brie, Camembert, goat cheese for cheeses; pasta primavera, ratatouille, and tomato-based pasta dishes for vegetarian options


Price: $42.00/bottle
