# Research Plan: Texas Hill Country Winery Travel Guidebook

## **1. Research Purpose Statement**
The purpose of this research is to better understand the intended users and their information needs in using the Texas Hill Country Winery Travel Guidebook. The project is designed to provide travelers with a guide for planning visits to wineries in the Texas Hill Country, reducing the need to search across multiple winery websites and other travel resources.

This research will help the team determine what information travelers actually need when choosing and planning winery visits, how that information should be organized, and which information needs to be verified directly with wineries or other reliable sources. Findings will guide the guidebook’s organization and content selection.

## **2. Research Questions**
Primary Research Questions

- Who are the primary users of the guidebook?
- How familiar are users with the Texas Hill Country and its wineries?
- What information do travelers need when planning a winery visit?
- What information do users look for first? And how do they prioritize the rest of their information needs?
- How important are hours, prices, wine selections, events, lodging, dining, transportation, and location?
- What information is difficult to find on existing winery websites?
- How do potential users currently research winery trips?

UX Questions

- What information should appear in an individual winery entry?
- What information should be available at a glance?
- Should the guidebook prioritize concise factual information, descriptive travel writing, or a combination?
- What terminology is helpful for people who have limited knowledge of wine? People with deep knowledge of wine? How do we best balance this different rhetoric?

Responsibility Questions

- What information should be verified before publication?
- Which sources are most reliable for each type of information?

Stakeholder Questions

- What information do winery representatives consider important or appropriate for travelers?

## **3. Method Description**

Conduct a search and document/website review of vineyards/wineries within or adjacent to the Texas Hill Country AVA to ultimately establish a list of 5-7 to include in the guidebook. Of those selected, find current wine lists and amenities offerings that would inform the details of the guidebook.

This material review will examine:

- Winery websites
- Tasting menus and wine lists (including pricing information)
- Hours and contact information
- Location via Google Maps
- Lodging and dining availability
- Existing Texas Hill Country travel guides and online reviews

Beyond collecting information for the final guidebook, this review can also identify patterns in how wineries communicate with visitors and identify information that is difficult to find or inconsistent between sources.

Additionally, in order to effectively communicate the available options to users of the guidebook, document review into current discourse on travel writing content strategy and rhetoric will be conducted.

## **4. Participant or Data Source Plan**
Participant sites will be sourced first from Texas Hill Country Wineries member wineries website, which presents a list of regional organizations.
https://texashillcountrywineries.org/pages/winery-listing-page

However, not all vineyards/wineries in the area are members. Other participants will be found through Google and investigated for appropriateness through online reviews, the organization’s online presence, and in some instances researcher site observations.

## **5. Observation and Review Protocol**

In reviewing online resources pertaining to each site, the researcher will evaluate certain parameters:

- Wines Available
- Pricing (perhaps value relative to pricing as well)
- Atmosphere
- Food
- Amenities
- Events & Activities
- Farming Practices
- Site-Specific Terroir

For site observations, the researcher may travel to the site or call a representative and attempt to fill the information gap, evaluating for the same parameters.

## **6. Ethics and Consent Considerations**

Verify important factual information: By using online, archivable resources, participant vineyards/wineries, worth considering is the veracity of their online information. Verifying the online information may be a responsibility of the researcher, as users will want a guidebook that they can rely on for accurate information and expertise in their private planning.

A consent consideration is the usage of art from featured locations, including photography, graphic design, and maps that may be used, and what copyright permissions would be involved if the guidebook were to be published.

Additionally, in referencing online customer reviews, reviews should be used primarily to identify key patterns rather than rely on individual opinions.

Research will:

- Prefer reliable, publicly available sources
- Verify factual information when possible
- Avoid making unsupported claims about businesses
- Avoid presenting promotional language as objective fact
- Respect copyright usage and image-licensing requirements
- Update or remove information when reliable sources or direct observation indicate that it is no longer accurate

The ethical purpose of the research is to improve the usefulness and accuracy of the guidebook, not to evaluate or rank individual wineries unfairly.
