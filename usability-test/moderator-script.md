## Introduction:

"Thank you for participating in our usability test. Our team is developing a travel guidebook for information related to wineries in the Texas hill country. This usability test is aimed at evaluating how effectively readers can navigate and use the document."

"During this test, you will be asked to complete five tasks using the current draft of our guidebook. These tasks are designed to simulate how a potential reader might use the guidebook when planning a visit to a winery in the Texas Hill Country."

"Please note that we are testing the effectiveness of the guidebook and any difficulties you experience will help us identify areas for improvement."

"As you complete the tasks, please think aloud by describing your thought process, what you're looking for, and whether anything seems confusing or incomplete."

"Your participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time. We will take notes about your experience, but we will not record audio, video, or personally identifying information."

"Do you have any questions before we begin?"

## Preliminary participant description questions:

I will begin with general questions to gauge your experience with wineries and trip planning:

- Have you ever visited a winery before?
- Have you ever used a travel guidebook to plan a trip?
- When planning a trip, what information do you consider most important?

## Usability Tasks:

We will now begin the usability test:

The participant will complete the following tasks using only the guidebook:
1.	Locate a winery that you would be interested in visiting and identify its location and operating hours.

2.	Compare that winery with another to evaluate their cost difference and wine selection

3.	Find accommodations near the winery of your choice for lodging and dining options

4.	After comparing wineries and seeing the availability and local accommodations, make a selection of which you would plan a trip to. Explain which information influenced your decision.

5.	Think through a plan to visit this winery. Discuss with the moderator what additional information you would need to execute this plan.

"Thank you for completing the tasks, I will now ask a few questions to evaluate your overall experience:"

## Post Test Questions:

- How easy or difficult was it to locate the information you needed throughout the guidebook?
- Did the organization of the guidebook feel intuitive? Were there any sections where you expected information to be located elsewhere?
- Did you find the amount of information sufficient, excessive, or lacking?
- If you could make a recommendation to change or improve the guidebook in any way, what would it be?
