# Usability Test Plan

## Purpose
The purpose of this usability test is to evaluate the effectiveness of our guidebook. The test will be aimed to examine whether the organization, presentation, and accessibility of the guidebook allow readers to locate and apply information efficiently. As our guidebook is intended to consolidate information that would otherwise require users to consult multiple websites, the test will also see what information is missing that users might need.

## Test Goals

Our usability test will address the following primary questions:
1.	**Navigation**: Can readers locate information about individual wineries without extended searching or confusion?
2.	**Information hierarchy**: Does the guidebook prioritize the information readers consider most important when planning a visit?
3.	**Clarity**: Are descriptions, pricing information, tasting notes, and operating details understandable to readers unfamiliar with the wineries?
4.	**Utility**: is the information we provide valuable and complete without the need for the user to go to outside resources for additional data?

## Participants

We will recruit 3–5 participants who represent potential users of the guidebook. The participants will be pooled from associates of the team members. We will record general, non-identifying information about the participants.

## Testing Method


We will conduct a moderated, task-based test using the current draft of our guidebook.

Each participant will complete tasks representing common travel planning activities and will be encouraged to vocalize their thought process while navigating the document.

The team member acting as a moderator will observe their actions without directing them towards correct navigation or decisions. 

# Needed Information

- Texas Hill Country Vineyard Travel Guidebook draft
- Moderator script or instructions
- Observation/note-taking form
- Post-test questions
- Brief test report
- Description of participants or reviewers
- Summary of findings
- Prioritized revision list
- Evidence supporting major recommendations

# Ethical Considerations
Participation will be voluntary. Participants will be informed of the purpose of the test and may discontinue participation at any time.
No personally identifying information will be included in the public GitHub repository. 
The team will not collect audio or video recordings.
