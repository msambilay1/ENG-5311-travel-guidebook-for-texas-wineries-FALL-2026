# Style Sheet for Vineyard Travel Guidebook

## Spelling and Tone

### Proofing Settings

*English*  (United States) spelling and grammar should be followed. When working in Microsoft Word, change the settings to:

- File > Options > Language > Office authoring languages and proofing > English (United States) in MS Word for 365 for Windows *or*
- Tools > Language > English (United States) in MS Word for 365 for MacOS

Alternatively, change the proofing language through:
- Review > Language > Set Proofing Language > English (United States)

### Tone

The text is reader-focused, so we will be employing active voice and confident phrasing that encourages the audience to take our suggestions for their itinerary. The tone itself will be professional and service-oriented towards the user. Factual information about the vineyards will be presented as accurately and complete as possible.

## Typography

### Headings
Sans Serif fonts - Roboto or Epilogue

- **Document title**: 20 (**bold**), centered or left-aligned
- **Chapter title**: 16 (capital), left-aligned
- **Heading 1**: 16 (**bold**), left-aligned
- **Heading 2**: 14 (**bold**), left-aligned
- **Heading 3**: 12 (**bold**), left-aligned
- **Heading 4**: 12 (*italics*), left-aligned
    > No indents for headings. Headings numbered in 1.1.1.1 format. 
- **Abstract**: 12 (*italics*)

### Body

Serif fonts - Times New Roman or Cambria
- **Text**: Size 12, left-aligned
- **Table**: Size 11, left-aligned
- **Notes**: Size 10, left-aligned

## Numbers

Numbers 0-9 should be spelled out as text, numbers 10 and above should be displayed as Arabic numerals.

If the sentence starts with a number, it should be spelled out as text. (i.e. "Seven days a week.")

Arabic numerals should be used for exact figures, such as measurements, prices, dates, and times. (i.e. 3 inches, 15 miles, $7.50, September 17, 2026, 10:30 AM)

## Visual Elements and Accessibility

Images (scenery, maps) and tables must be introduced in the preceding text *before* appearing on the page.

Any images to be used in the guidebook should be have an accompanying caption and alt text indicated in a companion Markdown file.
- Image captions should be placed *below* the visual element.
- Table titles should be placed *above* the table.

Maintain high contrast between text and backgrounds.

## Terminology
- **Business Names**: Capitalize the titles of official businesses referenced. ("Adega Vinho")
- **B.O.B/Private Label**: "Buyer's Own Brand." Use "Private Label" as opposed to "B.O.B." to avoid reader confusion.
- **Vintage**: Only capitalize when a part of a name. ("Offley Vintage Port" vs. "a vintage wine")
- **Vineyard/Winery**: Unless a part of an official name, use "vineyard" when referring to the agricultural fields that grow the grapevines, and "winery" when referring to the facilities that process and produce the wine.
